## The principles the walk says to hold

1. **A request is never dropped.** What it needs is said where it was asked, and it runs when the need is met.
2. **One list of who can read for you** — Claude in the room first when present, a suggested local model per job, hosted models by key — and nothing that needs a terminal in the main path.
3. **The user's words, not the engine's.** No ids, tiers, coordinates or slugs outside the inspector; one sentence in the status line.
4. **The default is the likely act.** Enter on writing reads it; on shapes it offers the act the drawing most likely wants — never a rename after a category.
5. **The field stays by the hand and whole.** Never over the minimap or chrome, never clipped.
6. **Offers are relevant or absent.**
7. **Help teaches the loop**; test plans live in the repo.
