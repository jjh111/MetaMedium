never needs the names, so nothing here limits what the license lets you do
with the code.

## Welcome, no need to ask

- **Forks and redistributions**, under a different name and without the
  logo or wordmark. Saying where it came from is good manners and welcome:
  *a fork of dyna.ink*, *based on dyna.ink*.
- **Plain statements of fact**, when they are true: *works with dyna.ink*,
  *built on dyna.ink*, *a board made in dyna.ink*, *compatible with
  dyna.ink*.
- **Talking about it**: articles, reviews, talks, lessons, videos and
  screenshots that name dyna.ink to refer to it.

## Please don't

- Use dyna.ink, DynaInk3D, or a name that could be mistaken for either, as
  the name of your product, service, company or domain.
- Use the logo or the wordmark to suggest that dyna.ink endorses, sponsors
  or made what you offer.
